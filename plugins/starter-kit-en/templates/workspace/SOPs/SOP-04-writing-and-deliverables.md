# SOP-04: Universal writing and deliverable rules

**Scope:** everyone

---

## Rule

These are the baseline quality expectations for outputs — regardless of who the owner is or what they do. (The owner's own style preferences go into `About Me/about-me.md` and override this where they conflict.)

### Writing

- **Concise, to the point.** Don't over-explain, don't pad.
- **No bullet-point salad.** Running text is better in most cases; use structure only where it really helps.
- **Plain English.** Short sentences, active voice, everyday words over jargon.
- **Avoid empty AI filler** — phrases like "delve into", "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in", "it's worth noting that", "a testament to".
- **Be concrete.** Numbers, examples, file names, dates — not generalities.

### Deliverables (material for clients / going outside)

- **Show the finished state, not the process.** Client-facing material shouldn't say "this was generated by the script" or "this changed compared to v2".
- **Don't suggest deletion or irreversible operations** unless explicitly asked.
- **Human tone.** A letter to a client should read like it was written by a person, not a template.

### Way of working

- **Ask when a request is unclear** — don't guess and head in the wrong direction.
- **Honest disagreement.** If you think a direction is wrong, say so — agreeing for its own sake has no value.
- **Flag paid tools first.** If a solution needs paid software, SaaS or an API, say so up front; by default choose the free / open-source option.

## Why

These are the most common quality killers in AI output: wordiness, the process leaking into the finished material, and wrong assumptions. Recorded in one place, every teammate can work to the same standard, and the owner doesn't have to ask for the same things again and again.

## How to apply

- Check any text or document output against this list before delivering it.
- If the owner's `About Me` states a more specific preference (e.g. formal or informal tone, specific terminology), it **overrides** this.
- This file itself grows through self-learning (`SOP-00`): as the owner's lasting style preferences emerge, they go here or into `About Me`.
