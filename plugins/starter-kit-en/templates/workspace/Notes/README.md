# Notes — notes and log

Your notes and the session log live here.

- **`session-log.md`** — a dated log of work sessions. Bea (scribe) writes it at the end of a session, append-only. This is how `/starter-kit-en:start-session` and Adam (`starter-kit-en:status`) know where the work left off. (A monthly split also works: `_session-log/YYYY-MM.md`.)
- **Other notes** — freely, by project or topic. Anything you want to keep that is neither a rule (that's an SOP) nor a task (that's Tasks).

Notes and the log are the system's "long-term memory": the AI does not remember between conversations, but the files stay.
